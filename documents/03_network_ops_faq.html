NCS Telco+ Network Operations Centre (NOC) — Internal FAQ

Document ID: NCS-NOC-FAQ-07 | Version: 1.2 | Effective Date: 1 May 2026 | Classification: Internal Training Use Only (Sample Data)

What are the incident severity levels?

NCS Telco+ classifies network incidents into four severity levels: Critical (P1), High (P2), Medium (P3), and Low (P4). A P1 incident means a total service outage affecting more than 1,000 subscribers, and must be acknowledged by an on-call engineer within 15 minutes of the alert firing.

How do I raise a P1 incident?

Call the NOC Emergency Line first, then log the incident in the ticketing system within 10 minutes of the call. Do not wait for the ticket to be logged before escalating — the phone call always comes first for P1 severity.

Who do I contact for a P1 escalation outside business hours?

Contact the on-call NOC Duty Manager, Rina Kapoor, at rina.kapoor@ncstelcoplus-sample.com or +91-9876543210. This contact is for P1 and P2 escalations only — P3 and P4 incidents should go through the standard ticketing queue and will be picked up during business hours.

What is the target resolution time for each severity level?

P1 incidents target resolution within 4 hours. P2 incidents target resolution within 8 business hours. P3 incidents target resolution within 3 business days. P4 incidents are addressed on a best-effort basis, typically within 10 business days.

What happens if a P1 incident is not resolved within the target time?

If a P1 incident remains unresolved after 4 hours, it is automatically escalated to the Network Operations Head, and a client-facing bridge call is scheduled within 30 minutes of that escalation.

How do I request a maintenance window?

Maintenance windows must be requested at least 5 business days in advance through the Change Management Portal, and require sign-off from both the NOC Duty Manager and the affected client's Account Manager before being scheduled.
